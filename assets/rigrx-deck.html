RIGRX

Emergency roadside help for trucks

A marketplace that connects broken-down truck drivers with vetted heavy-duty service companies — in minutes, not hours.

The problem

A breakdown on the highway is a race against the clock

Truckers stranded with a blown tire, dead reefer, or engine failure have no fast way to find qualified heavy-duty help nearby. Every hour off the road costs money — and calling around from the shoulder is slow, risky, and hit-or-miss.

Hard to find help

Heavy-duty service is specialized — a regular tow truck can't handle an 80,000 lb rig.

No trust signals

Drivers can't tell who's licensed, insured, or reliable when they're desperate.

Wasted time

Phone-tag with dispatchers while cargo spoils and delivery windows slip.

The solution

One request alerts every qualified company nearby

RIGRX matches each breakdown to approved service providers by service type, location, and truck class — then providers compete to respond. Live updates keep everyone in sync in real time.
